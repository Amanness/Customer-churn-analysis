# Customer Churn Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes customer churn using Python, Pandas, SQLite,
and data visualization to identify churn patterns, revenue impact,
and customer segments associated with churn.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention
- Identify churn patterns across customer segments
- Compare churn across subscription plans
- Analyze the relationship between customer support and churn
- Estimate the revenue impact of churn
- Identify areas for further investigation

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- SQLite
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings

- Churn Rate: 28.6%
- Retention Rate: 71.4%
- Monthly churn: 55.6%
- Annual churn: 8.3%
- Average Tenure: 1,548 days
- ARPU: 18.8
- Revenue loss due to churn: 74

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the increase in churn during September 2024,
  particularly in Karnataka.
- Examine monthly subscribers more closely.
- Review the Basic plan and its churn patterns.
- Prioritize high- and medium-risk customers based on their
  churn risk, CLTV, and complaint history.
- Investigate customer feedback and competitor activity.
